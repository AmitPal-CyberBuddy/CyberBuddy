How an assessment runs

01 · Target it

Paste any http(s) URL you are authorized to test.

02 · Collect evidence

Read-only checks capture the verdict, severity, and the raw header values behind every finding — nothing is uploaded or shared.

03 · Report it

Every result is a self-contained card: target, verdict, findings, evidence. Screenshot it for the deliverable.
